# 🚕 Traffic Tamasha – Indian Road Rush

An endless 3-lane driving game with an Indian street theme. Dodge auto rickshaws, scooters, painted trucks, stray cows, bullock carts, tractors, ambulances and more, all in your browser.

**▶ Play it:** https://viyona101-source.github.io/road-race-india/

## Choose your ride

| Ride | Top speed | Lives | Special |
|------|-----------|-------|---------|
| 🚗 Hatchback | 156 km/h | 3 | Sturdy and balanced |
| 🏍️ Sports Bike | 180 km/h | 2 | Fast, agile, bigger near-miss bonus |
| 🐎 Race Horse | 140 km/h | 3 | Huge jumps over traffic |

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Mobile |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Change lane | ← → or A / D | Swipe or tap left / right |
| Jump | Space, ↑ or W | Swipe up or JUMP button |
| Horn | H | – |
| Mute | M | 🔊 button |

## How to play

- Survive as long as you can. Speed increases over time.
- Near-misses give bonus points.
- Jump over potholes, speed breakers, cows and even buses. Landing on top of something counts as a crash.
- Watch out for the red **!** warning, since a wrong-way driver is coming.

## Run locally

It is a single self-contained file. Download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser.

## Tech

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on a canvas. No libraries, no external assets, and all sounds are generated in the browser.
